Privacy Policy

Sing Your Song · Hum Arranger · Last updated: 2026-11-21
The short version: your recordings and projects never leave your device. No servers, no accounts, no uploads.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend, no analytics SDK, no ad network and no crash reporting.

2. Microphone

Why we need itTo record the melody you hum and extract its pitch locally into editable notes.
Where the audio goesBrowser memory only. After processing you may export it to a local file; otherwise it is discarded when the page closes.
Is it uploaded?No. Nothing leaves the device unless you explicitly click “Share” to encode a project into a URL and send it yourself.
When you are askedOnly when you click Record. Declining does not affect other features.

3. Local storage

To remember your work between sessions the app uses the browser's localStorage to store the current project (notes, tracks, mixer settings, UI preferences).

  • Data lives in your own browser, under keys such as hum2song.project.v1
  • To erase it: click “New project” in the app, or clear site data in your browser
  • No copy exists on any server

4. Imported files

Audio files you import (mp3 / wav / ogg / m4a) are decoded in the browser and never uploaded.

5. Third-party components

Tone.jsAudio synthesis and playback engine (MIT). Bundled with the app.
Basic PitchPitch detection model (Apache-2.0). The model file is downloaded from a CDN on first use — download only, nothing sent. If offline, the app falls back to its built-in YIN algorithm.
Piano samplesSalamander Grand Piano (CC-BY 3.0, Alexander Holm). Bundled locally.
Other samplesUkulele, harp, ocarina, pipe organ, tenor sax (all CC0). Bundled locally.

6. Music you create

Arrangements generated from your humming belong to you, including commercial use. Sample recordings remain under their original licences (CC-BY / CC0).

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no age information.

8. Changes

Material changes will update the date above. Since no data is collected, we do not expect any.

9. Contact

Questions or data-related requests: GitHub Issues
